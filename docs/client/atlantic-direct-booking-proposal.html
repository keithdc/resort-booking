1. What we are building

You currently list on Airbnb. That relationship is ending and will not be renewed. This project replaces Airbnb with your own direct-booking website: you own the guest, the calendar, and the payment. It is not a marketplace and not a CRM. One organisation (you) can run many properties, each with many bookable houses.

Guests book in a straight line: dates and guests → free properties → name, email, phone → pay.

Surface Who uses it What it does
Guest website Travellers Photos, map, search, hold dates, pay (card / Apple Pay / Google Pay / bank transfer)
Staff admin You and your team Properties, houses, calendar, extras, refunds, bank-transfer checks, reports
API Both sites Availability, holds, prices, refunds. No booking maths in the website.

What this is not

  • Not Airbnb, Booking.com, or a multi-host marketplace
  • Not in-app guest–host chat (phone and email only)
  • Not PayPal or “pay later” (Klarna and similar) in the first live version
  • Not a package holiday (no flights bundled with the stay)

Launch shape

Two properties at launch, many houses each, more properties later without rebuilding the database. Guest calendars block past days. Staff calendars do not, so you can record history.

3. Requirements (product)

Guest

  • Multi-property catalogue, photos, OpenStreetMap, phone and email
  • Search by check-in, nights, guests; no identity documents stored
  • Hold: 15 minutes (card/wallets) or 48 hours (bank transfer)
  • Warning at five minutes left; expired hold keeps the form
  • Cookie consent before advertising pixels

Staff

  • Add properties and houses without a software release
  • Calendar of who is in-house; full stay (requests, notes, payment)
  • Verify bank receipts; approve on-request extras after the stay is paid
  • Cancellation / rebook settings per property, snapshotted onto the booking
  • Reports: nights, occupancy, revenue, filter by property

Money and EU operations

  • Never store card numbers. Stripe handles SCA / 3-D Secure
  • Bank transfer: unique reference; staff match the bank, not “accept the guest”
  • Dated holiday accommodation: the 14-day cooling-off right typically does not apply (Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU Article 16(l)). Checkout must say so clearly.
  • Your cancellation percentages are a commercial policy, not an EU-wide legal table
  • If you cancel the stay, the guest is refunded 100% of what they paid
  • Do not describe prepayment as French arrhes unless your lawyer says to

4. Architecture the board can read

Two websites, one API, one PostgreSQL database (the ledger), Redis only for short holds and jobs. Guest and staff are never one app with a “role switch.”

Piece Choice Why
Guest & admin UI Next.js + React + MUI SEO for listings; dense tools for staff; same language as the API
API NestJS + TypeScript Holds, overlap, refunds live here — not in the browser
Database PostgreSQL Reservations are rows with constraints, not a cache
Holds / jobs Redis 15-minute / 48-hour freezes and emails; never the booking history
Cards / wallets Stripe Apple Pay and Google Pay use the same fee as typing a card
Maps OpenStreetMap Directions via Google/Apple Maps links; no in-app chat

Model: Organisation → Property → House → Reservation. Every property-scoped row stores organisation and property ids so a third resort cannot leak into the first.

Technical guidelines (non-negotiable)

  • Strict TypeScript; no any
  • No availability or refund maths in React
  • Feature folders; every screen has an API and a SQL migration
  • Buttons 8px radius; colours from a theme, not hard-coded per page
  • Hosting, backups, and secrets stay off laptops in production

6. What is legal on each side (plain language)

Summary for discussion with counsel — not a substitute for advice.

Operator (you) — EU hospitality

  • You sell dated accommodation as a trader. Identify the company on the site (imprint).
  • You are the GDPR controller for guest names, emails, phones, and stays.
  • Charge VAT according to your member-state rules; we display amounts you configure.
  • Unfair contract terms: a hidden “keep 100% a month ahead” clause is risky. A clear stepped ladder is normal.
  • Package Travel Directive does not apply if we sell the stay only (no flight bundle).
  • You warrant that logos, photos, and names you give us do not infringe third-party rights.
  • You keep the relationship with Stripe and the bank. The Contractor does not hold guest funds.

Contractor (Keith Cordova)

  • Delivers software and configuration as an independent contractor, not your employee.
  • Does not give legal, tax, or investment advice.
  • Must not use your unpublished prices, guest lists, or brand files for other clients.
  • May use general skills and non-confidential methods on other work (see NDA residuals).
  • Open-source libraries stay under their licences (MIT, etc.); we do not assign those to you.
  • Production secrets (Stripe live keys) belong in your account, not in the public git history.
  • If hosting is in the Contractor’s name during prototype only, a written DPA is needed before real guests.

Means non-public information a Party discloses, including:

7.3 Exclusions

Information is not confidential if it is public without breach, already known without duty, independently developed, or required by law (with notice where lawful).

7.4 Use and care

A receiving Party shall use confidential information only for the Project, share it only with people who need it and are bound to keep it secret, and use at least reasonable care. The Operator is controller of guest data; the Contractor is a processor only if hosting or support so requires, in which case a GDPR Article 28 data-processing agreement will be signed before processing live guest data.

The Operator retains all rights in names, logos, and artwork it supplies. The Contractor receives a limited licence to use them solely to build and demonstrate the Project. Confidentiality lasts from the effective date until three (3) years after the Project ends, except trade secrets, which last as long as they remain secrets, and personal data, which follows GDPR.

7.8 Return

On written request, a Party shall return or destroy the other’s confidential materials, except copies required by law, backups that are overwritten in the ordinary cycle, or code assigned to the Operator.

No patent, copyright, or trademark licence is granted except as stated. Neither Party shall issue a press release about the Project without written consent, except to professional advisers under duty.

7.10 Residuals

Either Party may use residual knowledge retained in unaided memory (ideas, skills, know-how) that is not the other’s trade secret or personal data, provided it does not copy documents or code.

7.11 Remedies

Breach may cause harm that money cannot fix. A Party may seek an injunction in addition to other remedies, without limiting damages where the law allows.
